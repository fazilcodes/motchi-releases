## What is it?

Windows has no built-in Dynamic Island. Motchi is an independent, Dynamic Island-style notch for Windows 10 and 11: a small black pill at the top centre of your screen. It stays quiet until something matters (a timer, a reminder, a meeting, the song playing), and when you hover it, or press **Alt + N**, it opens into a calm workspace.

It is not an Apple product and is not affiliated with Apple.

## What it does

**Free, forever**
- Today's tasks with quick add
- Gentle reminders that drop from the notch (Snooze and Done)
- Focus timer, with optional focus sounds
- Water and mood check-ins, achievements, notes
- Voice typing that runs on your PC (one-time model download, offline after that)
- Calendar import: meetings from an .ics link or file, read-only
- Clipboard history (Clips), off until you turn it on
- A file library with folders, and a card for new screenshots
- A mini now-playing player beside the notch
- Small, medium or large notch; Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit

**Motchi Pro: $4.99, once, for one PC** (no subscription)
- The planner, long-range insights and reports, Mood over time
- The full library, your own looks and layout
- AI usage view for Claude Code, Codex and similar CLIs

If Pro ever lapses you are back on Free. Nothing is locked, hidden or deleted, and you can always export your data.

## Install

1. Download **[Motchi-Setup.exe](https://github.com/fazilcodes/motchi-releases/releases/latest/download/Motchi-Setup.exe)** from the latest release (about 100 MB, no admin rights needed).
2. Run it. **Windows may show a SmartScreen warning**, see below.
3. A small black notch appears at the top of your screen. Hover it, or press **Alt + N**.

### About the SmartScreen warning

Motchi is new and made by one person, and the website installer is **not yet signed with a paid code-signing certificate**, so browsers and Windows can't verify it and warn about new apps.

- **In your browser:** open the downloads list and click **Download unverified file** (or **Keep**).
- **In Windows:** when it says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

A Microsoft Store version is coming; Microsoft checks and signs Store apps. Updates in the website version are checked against Motchi's own release signature before they install.

## Privacy

- Your tasks, notes, moods and files stay in an encrypted database on your PC.
- The free version makes no network calls except the ones you switch on: update checks, voice typing's one-time model download, feedback you choose to send, an optional email at setup, and a calendar link you add (it contacts only that calendar's address).
- Pro checks its licence with the Motchi server from time to time.
- No analytics inside the app, no ads.

Full details: [motchi.xyz/privacy](https://motchi.xyz/privacy)
